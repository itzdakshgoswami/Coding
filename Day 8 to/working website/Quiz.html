Travelling by train is a wonderful and exciting experience. Last summer, I travelled by train with my family to visit my grandparents. We reached the railway station early and waited eagerly for the train. When the train arrived, we boarded it and found our seats. As the train moved, I enjoyed watching green fields, rivers, villages, and small towns through the window. The sound of the train and the cool breeze made the journey enjoyable. I also talked and shared snacks with my family. After a few hours, we reached our destination safely. It was a memorable journey that I will always cherish.
